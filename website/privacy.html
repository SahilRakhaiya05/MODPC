Privacy Policy

Privacy that matches a moderator workflow.

This policy explains what MODPC may collect, how that data is used, and how it is handled when you use the website or install the moderation workspace in Reddit.

At a glance

  • Effective date: May 27, 2026
  • Core principle: collect only what the workspace needs
  • Live mode: real Reddit data only

Data handling

MODPC is designed to operate inside Reddit’s moderation environment. Sensitive actions remain gated and scoped to the subreddit you moderate.

Section 1

Information we may collect

  • Account and moderation context made available by Reddit for the active subreddit.
  • Workspace settings, installed subreddit references, and moderator preferences required for the app to function.
  • Audit events and workflow data created when you use MODPC features.
Section 2

How we use information

We use data to render the workspace, show queue and mod-log summaries, save settings, support moderation actions, and improve the experience for your subreddit.

Where you enable optional integrations, we may also use submitted prompts or documents to generate assistance within the product.

Section 3

Sharing and disclosure

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We may share data with Reddit services and configured providers strictly to run the product.
  • We may disclose information if required to comply with law, policy enforcement, or platform safety obligations.
Section 4

Storage and retention

Workspace data may be stored in the app’s configured backing services, such as Redis, so the moderation desk can persist settings, installs, and activity history.

Retention should be limited to what is necessary for the product to operate and for moderators to review recent actions.

Section 5

Your choices

  • You can stop using the app or uninstall it from the subreddit.
  • You can change workspace settings in the app when available.
  • You can avoid optional integrations if you do not want external processing.
Section 6

Security

MODPC uses permission checks and subreddit scoping to reduce access to moderation data. No system is perfectly secure, so you should review sensitive actions before confirming them.

Section 7

Children’s privacy

This product is intended for moderators and community administrators. It is not designed for children, and it should not be used to knowingly collect information from children except as permitted by applicable law and platform rules.

Section 8

Contact and updates

Questions about privacy can be sent through the project contact channel or repository. We may update this policy when the product changes, and the page will reflect the latest version.
